## 1. Comparing myself to the beginning of the year

I believe, in the beginning of this year I was a lot more timid. In CSSE I found coding very frusturating and saw towards the end I was doing almost the bare minimum to pass. I saw the other table however working on something cool, the platformer game and I wanted to work on something cool like that. Going into the new year in CSP, I was a little unsure about myself but I was looking for something more to do. I didn't want to just be someone "getting by" in the class, I wanted to have some impact and I wanted to take responsibility. 

Towards the end of this Trimester I believe I've been able to take a lot more opportunities to try something new than I would've in the beginning. For example helping students with the tools set up during the "quiz" where we had to prove we could run make. Then I tried to connect the class through one period 4 repo that would try to merge all the teams in the period's changes into one repo before pushing to main pages. The goal was try to simplify merging to pages and though it wasn't successful, I learnt a lot about working in big teams.

As we transitioned to the end of the Trimester I used what I learnt from the second part of the trimester, being the JS and Python lessons, to help work with my planet and team. I was the assistant scrum master of the cybersecurity planet and I'm somewhat satisfied with how it went. I believe I could've organized the team a little better as we were a little disorganized throughout our planet. I think overall there was a lack of effort until the last minute and also a lot of confusion between teammates due to a lack of communication which I could've handled better.

Overall I've changed a lot in how I approach the challenges given to me, from just trying to get by to actually trying to make a meaningful impact. I think my view on handling tasks has changed the most and my effort to actually do something about it.
